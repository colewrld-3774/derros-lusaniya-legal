## Information We Collect

We collect information needed to run food ordering and delivery:

- Account information: name, email address, phone number, Firebase user ID, account role, and email verification status.
- Order information: ordered items, quantities, prices, discounts/offers, delivery address, payment method label, order status, order dates, cancellation reasons, reviews, ratings, and remarks.
- Location information: customer delivery location and rider location while a delivery is active.
- Notification information: device push token, notification history, order IDs, notification type, and notification delivery state.
- App activity: actions such as placing, accepting, cancelling, delivering, completing, reviewing, and managing orders.
- Technical information: app/device identifiers and diagnostics collected by Firebase or Google services used by the app.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create and manage user accounts.
- Let customers place and track orders.
- Let riders accept and deliver assigned orders.
- Let admins and super admins manage orders, menu items, offers, categories, riders, reviews, and reports.
- Send order, rider, cancellation, delivery, offer, and account notifications.
- Calculate revenue, sales, rider delivery history, and operational reports.
- Improve reliability, security, and performance.
- Prevent abuse, unauthorized access, and fraud.

## Location Data

Location is used for delivery. Customers may provide a delivery location, and riders may share location while handling an active delivery so customers and admins can track delivery progress.

The app should not request background location unless background rider tracking is added and clearly disclosed. The current app release should use foreground location for delivery features.

## Notifications

The app sends notifications for order and delivery activity, including placed orders, accepted orders, rider assignment, order status changes, cancellations, reviews, offers, and menu updates. We do not use notifications for unrelated spam.

## Phone Calls And WhatsApp

Admins, super admins, and riders may tap phone numbers to contact customers or riders. Calling and WhatsApp actions are user initiated and open the phone dialer or WhatsApp outside the app.

## Sharing Information

We use Firebase and Google services to operate authentication, database storage, cloud messaging, app configuration, and app delivery. These providers process data as service providers for app functionality.

We may share information if required by law, to protect users, to prevent fraud or abuse, or to operate delivery services requested by the user.

We do not sell personal information.

## Data Security

We use Firebase Authentication, Firestore security rules, HTTPS/TLS network transport, role-based app access, and limited admin tools to protect user data. Privileged server credentials must never be stored inside the mobile app.

## Data Retention

We keep account, order, delivery, notification, review, and report data for as long as needed to provide the service, support users, meet legal or tax obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the business.
