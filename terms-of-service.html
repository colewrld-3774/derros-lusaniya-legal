Prices, offers, discounts, and availability may change. The app displays order totals in UGX. If a payment issue or pricing error occurs, Derros Lusaniya may contact the user or cancel the order where necessary.

Riders must only access delivery information needed for assigned work. Admins and super admins must use management tools only for legitimate restaurant operations.

Users must not abuse notifications, create fake orders, harass riders or customers, attempt unauthorized access, manipulate roles, or interfere with app systems.
